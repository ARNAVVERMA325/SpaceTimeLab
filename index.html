Spacetime Lab — Milestone 1: Minkowski baseline

Backward null-geodesic ray tracing through flat spacetime. Deliberately undramatic: a correct pipeline in Minkowski bends nothing, so the sky grid arrives exactly as a pinhole camera would project it. Curved grid lines are rectilinear projection of a sphere, not light deflection — see Image projection below.
