The photon follows a null geodesic of the chosen spacetime. The background grid is a visualization mapping on a sphere at fixed coordinate radius, not a physical emitting surface. The accretion disk is an analytical Novikov–Thorne model radiating as a local blackbody: no plasma, no scattering, no returning radiation, no radiative transfer, and not a prediction of any observed image. This is the CPU reference path, traced in parallel Web Workers; the assembled image is bit-identical to a single-threaded render.
