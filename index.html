Spacetime Lab

Backward null-geodesic ray tracing through a chosen spacetime. One pipeline, three scenes: flat spacetime bends nothing, Schwarzschild bends light into a shadow and an Einstein ring, and a Novikov–Thorne thin disk is coloured by the frequency shift of the rays that reach it. Every number on the right comes from the render you are looking at.

Data hierarchy

The photon follows a null geodesic of the chosen spacetime. The background grid is a visualization mapping on a sphere at fixed coordinate radius, not a physical emitting surface. The accretion disk is an analytical Novikov–Thorne model radiating as a local blackbody; it does not model plasma, scattering, returning radiation or radiative transfer, and it is not a prediction of any observed image. This is the CPU reference path, traced in parallel Web Workers: the assembled image is bit-identical to a single-threaded render, and interactive frame rates are a separate milestone with its own cross-validation gate.
